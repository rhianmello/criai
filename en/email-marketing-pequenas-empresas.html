Build a list that actually expects your emails

A small business might offer a helpful price guide or a useful checklist through a form. Prospects should understand what information they are providing and what messages they will receive.

📝

Simple form

Ask for the minimum details, state the purpose of the signup, and avoid hidden subscriptions or purchased lists.

🗂️

Useful segmentation

Separate new visitors, returning customers and distinct interests rather than sending everyone the same message.

📬

Relevant follow-ups

Send helpful content before making offers and always provide a clear unsubscribe mechanism.

Example: a four-email sequence for a service provider

  1. Welcome: deliver what the visitor requested and explain what subsequent emails will cover.
  2. Common question: answer a specific concern or problem your customers frequently mention.
  3. How to evaluate solutions: share practical criteria and common mistakes, without exaggerating results.
  4. Relevant invitation: offer a consultation or product with a clear, optional next step and an unsubscribe link.

Check these costs before subscribing

  • Number of contacts and contact growth limits
  • Number of emails permitted per month
  • Form and landing page features
  • Integrations, deliverability and support
  • Annual agreement compared with monthly billing
  • Unsubscribes, permissions and compliance
