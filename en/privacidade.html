Privacy at CRIAÍ

This notice describes how our public website handles language selection, optional analytics, links to suppliers, third-party media and email purchase requests.

Last updated: November 16, 2026. This notice reflects the current version of the website and must be updated as new checkout or account functionality becomes available.

1. Information you provide

No public account registration is required for the guide. Once checkout is configured, we collect the purchase email address and chosen PDF language to create a private order record, verify payment and provide customer support. Payment details are collected by Mercado Pago, where available, not by our website. Only after verification with the payment provider do we generate a temporary download link to the purchased PDF. If checkout is unavailable you may contact us by email. Your browser's session storage holds the purchase identifier and random access token to recover the delivery after returning from the payment provider; the access token is not transmitted as an analytics event. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

2. Analytics

CRIAÍ has a Google Analytics 4 measurement ID configured (G-RG57Q34VB0). We only load the statistics tag after you choose to allow analytics. When enabled, page visits and interactions such as clicks on affiliate links may be measured. Our analytics events are designed not to submit names, email addresses or payment details. You may review and withdraw consent using the privacy preferences control. Withdrawing consent prevents new events from being sent by our implementation after reloading, but does not erase information Google collected previously. Analytics cookies are configured for the CRIAÍ subdomain rather than our sister site's domain.

3. Language preference

CRIAÍ can show pages in Portuguese or English. On your first visit, the browser's preferred language may determine whether the site opens its English version. This does not require GPS location or an IP-based country lookup. Choosing a language saves a functional preference in your browser's local storage under criai_language_v1 so the site can respect your choice on later visits. Language selection does not send that value to our servers as a separate analytics event.

4. Hosting and technical records

The hosting provider may process technical access logs, including an IP address and browser information, for operating and securing the website under its own policies.

5. Affiliate links and third-party checkouts

Some links go to Hotmart and to the software supplier. Those third parties may use attribution cookies, process contact information, and handle purchases under their own privacy notices and terms. A click is not proof of a sale or a commission. Our own guide does not yet have an automated checkout.

6. Videos and external images

Supplier demonstrations may link to YouTube. Third-party players can process data when loaded. Some promotional banners on the Portuguese site are hosted at i.imgur.com; requesting them may transmit technical information such as your IP address and browser details to that host.

7. Updates to this notice

We will need to update this notice when the site begins handling customer accounts, purchases, new cookies or additional analytics providers. The terms in effect with external suppliers are governed by their own documents.

8. Contact and choices

For privacy questions, contact suporte@xrmovi.com.br. You may continue browsing without optional analytics.
