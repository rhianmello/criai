Step one: identify a real problem

Start with the problem, not the software. Look for repetitive tasks, recurring errors or frustrating processes. Choose a specific audience instead of trying to serve everyone.

  1. Observe a repeated pain point in a particular type of business.
  2. Identify the people who experience it and ask them how they handle it today.
  3. Describe a concrete result that can be verified.

Exercise: “I help [audience] achieve [result] without [main obstacle].” Test the sentence with at least three potential customers.
